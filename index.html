About Bioinventics

Research, consultancy and computational drug discovery from Rajshahi, Bangladesh.

Our Mission

Shorten the path from a disease to a safe, potent lead compound using reproducible computational methods.

Our Vision

A research centre from Bangladesh whose computer-designed molecules reach the clinic.

Why nature

Natural products are secondary metabolites of plants, fungi, bacteria and other organisms, and remain one of the richest sources of medicines. Vinblastine, a cancer drug from the Madagascar periwinkle Catharanthus roseus, is a famous example.

Why computers

Extracting and testing every natural compound in the lab is slow and costly. Computer-aided drug design with docking and molecular dynamics screens candidates much faster and sends only the best ones to the bench.

Our mark. The laptop and atom stand for computation, the mortar and leaves for natural products, and the DNA for genomics. Together they make one lab, summed up in our motto: Computer is your lab, now execute experiments.
